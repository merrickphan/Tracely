## How to mark

Fill in the `you:` line for any source I got wrong. **Leave it blank if you agree.**
Write one of:

  rel   — actually evidence for THIS claim as phrased
  marg  — right topic, but does not evidence the claim as phrased
  irr   — not about this claim at all

Partial is fine — even one claim helps. Add a note after the verdict if
you want to say why. Save the file and tell me when you are done.

The `marg` calls are the ones most worth disagreeing with. A paper can be
squarely on the topic and still not support the specific sentence.
